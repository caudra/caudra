Preview 6 adds autopilot, an experimental mode that answers permission prompts, questions, and ready plans for you while a session works toward its goal.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Close every other Caudra session before you start Preview 6.** On its first start, Preview 6 upgrades the session database in your [state directory](https://caudra.ai/docs/configuration/#directory-layout), and the upgrade needs exclusive access. This includes other terminals, editor sessions over ACP, and `caudra storage` commands. While any of them still has the database open, Preview 6 does not start. It lists the open sessions to close instead, and nothing is changed.
>
> The upgrade keeps your sessions and saves a copy of the database from before it as `caudra.db.v23.bak` next to `caudra.db`. Preview 5 and earlier cannot open the upgraded database, and `caudra rollback` restores only the previous binary, not the database.

### Autopilot (experimental)

- **Keep a session moving.** With `experimental.autopilot` on, `/autopilot <goal>` sets a goal and hands what would wait for you to a separate delegate model: permission prompts, the `question` tool, questions asked in prose, a plan that is ready, and the stalls that end a run, such as a goal loop cap or a provider outage. Autopilot ends when the goal is met or impossible, a budget runs out, or you stop it. See [Autopilot](https://caudra.ai/docs/autopilot/).
- **Prompts it never approves.** The delegate approves a permission prompt once and never adds a rule. Prompts that plan mode raises, tools that always prompt, protected files and commands, and every prompt in a session that content screening flagged are only ever denied with guidance or left to you.
- **Budgets.** Each engagement may start 100 turns without you by default. `max_cost` and `max_minutes` under `[autopilot]` add spend and time limits, and edits apply from the next turn.
- **You stay in charge.** Every decision is a transcript row, and a footer chip opens a panel with the budget and recent decisions. A key or click on a card autopilot is deciding makes it yours. Esc Esc, Ctrl-C while the agent works, or `/autopilot off` stops autopilot. An item it leaves to you raises an urgent notification and a blocked pane status.
- **Headless and CI.** `caudra -p --autopilot "/goal <condition>"` runs unattended, and a prompt autopilot leaves to a human is denied. SDK clients engage it with `--autopilot` or the `autopilot_start` control. See [Headless Mode](https://caudra.ai/docs/headless/#autopilot).
- **Its own model job.** Decisions use the new Autopilot job, which follows Best unless you bind it in `/model` or with `/autopilot model`. The lifetime view in `/usage` lists its spend under `autopilot`.

### MCP tool cards

- **Readable results.** An MCP result that is a JSON document is drawn indented and coloured, and other text is drawn as markdown. Images, audio, and resources become labelled rows. The model receives the same text as before.
- **Cleaner header.** A card reads `server › tool` with the call's arguments beside it, and opening the card shows them in full.

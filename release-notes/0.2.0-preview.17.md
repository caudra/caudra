### xAI

- **xAI subscription quotas in `/usage`.** Shows included-credit usage and its reset time, plus prepaid and on-demand balances when reported. Free-tier rolling 24-hour restrictions appear after a refused request, with the observed token counts and timestamp. These observations do not claim an exact reset time and clear after a successful request for that model or after 24 hours.
- **Stop retrying exhausted xAI free usage.** A free-usage exhaustion response no longer triggers repeated short retries. Automatic account selection can switch to another eligible account. Pinned accounts remain pinned and report the restriction.

### Logs

- **Wrapped `/logs` navigation and drag-to-copy.** With wrapping on, the wheel, the scrollbar, and the page keys move through `/logs` row by row instead of snapping back, a record taller than the pane can be read to its end, and the selected record stays highlighted. Dragging over log text now selects it and copies it on release, with wrapped lines kept whole.

### Shell

- **More shell history, the same training limit.** Caudra now retains up to 100,000 local shell runs, up from 20,000, preserving more duration and optional CPU measurements for evaluation. ETA models still train on at most the newest 20,000 runs, so retaining more history does not increase the training sample limit. Runs already pruned are not recovered. CPU measurements remain collection-only.

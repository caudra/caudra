Preview 10 lets MCP snippets ask the decision engine typed questions and rerank long results before the model reads them, moves workflow `decide()` to the same question shape, the one OpenAI Decisions uses, copies what you drag over in `/btw` and the question form, and fixes subscription image generation after GPT-5.5 retired from Codex.

> [!WARNING]
> **If your workflows call `decide()`, rewrite their questions when you update.** Questions are now a list instead of a map keyed by ID, so each question names itself with `name`. `noul` questions become `predicate` questions, and `criteria` becomes `choices` for a choice and `levels` for a score. Answers come back as `answers`, a list in question order, and `by_name`, keyed by name, and a predicate's probability is now in `probability` rather than `noul`. A workflow that still passes the old shape fails at its first `decide()` call with a catchable error, and so does a run started before the update when you resume it, even at a call it had already answered. See [Typed decisions](https://caudra.ai/docs/workflows/#typed-decisions).

### MCP

- **Decisions and reranking in snippets.** With the decision engine on, an `mcp` snippet can call `decide()` for typed answers about its data, and `rerank()` to keep only the candidates relevant to a query, best first, for up to 256 candidates. A snippet can rank 200 issue titles and return the ten that matter, so the rest never reach the model. The model is told how to write questions and queries that get reliable answers, such as naming the field each question judges and describing every option. Calls need no approval, work in plan mode, show as rows on the card, and appear in the decision log under the feature `mcp`. See [Decisions and reranking](https://caudra.ai/docs/mcp/#decisions-and-reranking).
- **Decision answers you can read at a glance.** A `decide()` row on the `mcp` card shows its verdicts on one line, and always shows each question below it with a bar per option, the likeliest highlighted, and a warning when the winner is uncertain. A `rerank()` row always shows the candidates it kept, best first, under the labels they were given. Click the row's footer to see the JSON the snippet received; selecting any part of the row copies that JSON, in either view. In `/decisions`, Recent draws logged answers the same way, and `j` switches them to JSON.

### Workflows

- **`decide()` takes OpenAI Decisions questions.** A choice option or a score level can carry a description, as a map with `value` or `label` and `description`, and each answer lists the probability of every option or level. Both decision protocols return the same shape. `rerank()` is unchanged.

### Transcript

- **Pictures in the transcript.** `view_image` and `image_generate` cards, Markdown images that point at local files, and prompts with attached images now show the picture as a thumbnail. Terminals that support Kitty graphics get the full-resolution picture, and other terminals get coloured blocks, two by two pixels to a cell, or two by three with `inline_images = "sextants"` in terminals and fonts that draw sextants. Click a picture to open it at full size, then press `o` to open it in your system viewer, `w` to open it in the workbench, or `y` to copy its path. Images attached to a draft show as `[Image #1]` chips you can click, and the workbench shows image files instead of the binary file notice. Markdown images are never fetched from the network. Set `[ui] inline_images` to `"blocks"`, `"sextants"`, `"kitty"`, or `"off"` to choose. See [Images](https://caudra.ai/docs/markdown/#images).

### Selection

- **Drag to copy in `/btw` and the question form.** Dragging over a `/btw` thread copies the passage when you let go, the way the transcript copies: within one answer as its Markdown, and across questions and answers as sections headed `User`, `Assistant`, `Thinking`, and `Error`, without the footer, the follow-up field, or a line break at every wrapped row, and the passage stays selected while the answer streams and scrolls. `Ctrl+Y`, `Ctrl+C`, and the footer's copy take the selection before the whole answer. In the question form, a drag starts anywhere, including on an option, and copies instead of picking what it ends on. A drag in the answer box selects and copies its text, and `Ctrl+C` copies a selection before it stops the agent. The form's border is no longer copied with the text. See [Commands](https://caudra.ai/docs/commands/) and [Keybindings](https://caudra.ai/docs/keybindings/#form).

### Image generation

- **ChatGPT subscription image generation uses a supported model.** Image requests now run through GPT-6.1 Sol instead of GPT-5.5, which OpenAI retired from Codex with ChatGPT sign-in on October 14. This fixes the unsupported-model error without changing your configured image model or switching to API billing.
